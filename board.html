Board of Directors

Suma Dixit S.Y

Secretary

Bharatanatyam dancer and teacher. Born in Shivamogga, Karnataka, in 1973. She became a disciple of Guru Smt. Pushpa Krishnamurthi at the age of six and learnt the Pandanallur style. Guidance followed from Vidwan Venkata Rao, then Guru Ravi Dhatar and Guru Geetha Dhatar for junior and senior levels respectively, and she took vidwat classes from the late Padmini Rao and Suma R.

Education

  • Bachelor of Business Management (B.B.M.), National College, Shivamogga, 1992–1995
  • Legum Baccalaureus (LL.B.), National College, Shivamogga, 1996–1999

Experience

Founder and Head, Anu Kala Kendra (2003 – present). She has tutored around 150+ Bharatanatyam students over more than 22 years.

Performances

  • Chikmaglur: Ganapati Seva Samiti; Datta Jayanthi Utsava; Rama Mandira Temple
  • Channarayapatna: Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan
  • Shivamogga: Kuvempu Rangamandira Nrutyotsava 2007; Sri Ganapati Sacchidananda Ashrama
  • Mysore: Sri Ganapati Sacchidananda Ashrama; Natya Samskruthi Kalanikethana Nrityotsava
  • Chennai: Kalai Maghal Sabha
